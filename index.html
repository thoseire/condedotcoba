How to Play

  • Connect the stars by tapping or clicking two stars in sequence.
  • Remove lines by tapping or clicking them. Use Undo Line to remove the last line or Clear Map to start over.
  • Choose a line colour before drawing. Line colours do not affect your answer.
  • Check the star colours. They mark the three brightest stars in order: Blue (1st), Orange (2nd), and Red (3rd).
  • Submit your observation when you are ready. Your answer will only be checked after you press Submit Observation.
  • Revise if needed. If your pattern is incorrect, adjust your lines and submit again before the timer runs out.
