How to Play

  • Tap or click one star, then another, to connect them.
  • Tap a connected line to remove it. Use Undo Line or Clear Map if needed.
  • Choose a line colour before drawing; colour does not affect your answer.
  • Press Submit Observation when you are ready. The map is not checked while you draw.
  • If the answer is incorrect, revise your lines and submit again before the timer ends.
